Python is a high-level, interpreted programming language known for its exceptional readability and clear syntax. 
Developed by Guido van Rossum and first released in 1991, Python emphasizes code readability, allowing programmers to express concepts in fewer lines of code than possible in languages such as C++ or Java. 
Its core philosophy is summarized by the Zen of Python, which includes principles like "Beautiful is better than ugly" and "Simple is better than complex." 
Because of its gentle learning curve and powerful standard library, Python has become one of the most popular programming languages in the world.
